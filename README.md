**Preamble:** Continuing the sequence of mathematical oncology and systems biology investigations that began with Thesis Zero (BSc Carica papaya AgNP, Nile University 2022), this forty-sixth volume expands the dynamical systems approach to tumour-immune interactions. Building on the immunometabolic frameworks established in T10 and the topological stability paradigms of T17, this work interrogates the chronobiological dimensions of immune surveillance.

## Non-claims
This manuscript is a theoretical computational model. It does not constitute medical advice, nor does it propose clinical interventions for shift workers or oncology patients. The differential equations herein describe idealized cellular kinetics and should not be construed as validated predictive tools for human pathophysiology without prospective clinical trial validation.

## The Problem in One Sentence
While epidemiology establishes circadian disruption as a probable carcinogen, there exists no dynamical systems model coupling a circadian oscillator to Natural Killer (NK) cell tumour-kill kinetics to quantify the surveillance window.
